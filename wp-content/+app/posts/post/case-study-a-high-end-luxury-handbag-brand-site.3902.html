What does it mean to be prestigious? Some may say, to have a certain allure; some may say, to be what others cannot be. In this case study for an unreleased site, designed by a certain group of biking, beary people, I play the role of overzealous construction worker, bringing to life an experience emulating opulence & excess through the web.

Products

The meat & potatoes of a shop site. Of course, that means no expense can be spared when creating the experience associated with it, including view transitions (from scratch, compatible everywhere), async categories, & fancy pants sticky progressive blurs.

Services

A "services" page is usually super simple, but that's no fun isn't it? Though, it's always pretty boring reading about some organisation's particulars through a screen--what if, we pretended it wasn't a screen?

About

Finally, we come to the final plain information page, the last chance to sell the movement, & set the brand up for completion.

Reflection

This was actually a scrapped project mid-way into development, which is a major disappointment. However, I learnt a lot from it, & it pushed my graphics programming skills far forward. Curtains ain't easy to program, okay? If I had to go back & remake this project, I would happily have at it. I learnt so much & I'm glad I wasted my time.
