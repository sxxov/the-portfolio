Ever find yourself being annoyed, being pressured, being nagged by the little screens all around you? You're not alone, Deceptive Design (fka. Dark Patterns) are an epidemic that has been silently sweeping across the UI design sphere.

You know, the pop-ups that tell you that you've got a voucher that is about to expire, even though you've just opened up your silly shopping app; the extremely small "x" buttons every time you go to close the silly ad; the ease of putting in money into the silly gambling app, compared to the asinine process you have to go through to not lose everything & end up in a ditch with your brains shot.

You know, the usual day-to-day technological hurdles... right?

What if, these problems aren't just the cost of being connected to the world's, & potentially the universe's most advanced information highway. What if, we could quantify & actually sniff out when a bad actor is subversively manipulating you?

This, my friend, is how Deceitville came into existance.

Minigames

What's a game without the actual content? To answer an age old question:

Would you rather have unlimited bacon, & no video games,
or unlimited video games, & no video games.

Each level within Deceitville describes a type of deceptive design, with an information sheet that links out to peer-reviewed research to boot. Some may be less obvious than others, but isn't that the fun with real deceptive designs too?

Reflection

This was a dream project, not in a way that it went completely smoothly, but that it gave me the space to truly grow as an artist & enginner. Sure there were trials, sure there were tribulations, but the good kinds. In hindsight, all the challenges I faced were offset by what was learnt, like how to force-crash a browser with a bug that was supposed to be fixed.

Honestly, I'm writing this writeup long after this project has finalised, so to pose with a wish for the future would be extremely disingenious. Let's just say, this project was really a catalyst to my love at deep space, with the space being the Web & the love being my fist.
