Have you ever been through a mid-life crisis? What about a quarter-life crisis? Okay, what about just a crisis? If you have, you might have felt life's monotony in your face, fist first. At some point, you can distill living into working, having fun, & sleeping. However, trying to keep up with these aspects of life, you might feel like your time is never enough.

Succide is about precise balance, & it shows you how a lack any core pillars in life, will kill you in a slow & boring way.
