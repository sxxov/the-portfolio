It isn't everyday you get the opportunity to work on a historical rebrand of a historical entity. Designed by the furry folks of biking with bears, I got to work on this project during my tenure there as the main developer. There were no shortages of challenges, though I'd say the final product shows triumph in the work.

Businesses

Each of Sime's businesses are presented in their own way, under a set structure. This meant that the scaffolding holding all the information had to be both malleable & rigid enough, to ensure the content could be presented consistently & completely.

Timelined history

This component was the one that took the longest to perfect. Initially envisioned to contain a lengthy history with images & descriptions, the timeline was built to be collapsible, draggable, & responsive to boot.

News & Story Migrations

This was another part of the project that required planning--migrating 14 years of content from their old site to this shiny new one, whilst preserving translations, images, & files. A large scale scraping effort commenced, which eventually distilled itself into the site through a custom plugin that digested the posts & their metadata.

Reflection

This was a difficult project, not only due to its scale, but due to lots of technical hurdles coming along. Though, I definitely learnt a lot, especially for critical site features like search, pagination, filtering, & fragmented content.
