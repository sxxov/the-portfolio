A design-focused single-page site, for an up & coming motion designer in the ident/intro mograph space. Created in the span of a few weeks, with the main tech being a single Lottie animation being seeked around. This was also one of the first sites I know of that attempts to do image-sequence scrubbing by scroll, hence a from-the-ground-up implementation of buffering per-frame video using multiple strategies, including a worker-based, a few fallback <video>s.
