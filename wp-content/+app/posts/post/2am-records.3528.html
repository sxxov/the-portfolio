2AM Records is a bootstrap, local streetwear brand from Malaysia, focusing on bringing their own minimalist & movement-inspired series of wearable statement pieces. This brings us to what a web presence means to them--another vessel for showcasing their aura, & a tool to facilitate sales that power said aura.

Whilst this was spun as a revamp project for the collective, it was more like a reconstruction & ressurection based on the remnants of a previous site. Their online presence has mostly been restricted to Instagram ever since the retirement of said old site, though to their credit, this approach worked for a surprisingly long time considering their growth along the years.

Now though, 2AM is ready for a fresh new look, a fresh new way of bridging the consumer<>provider gap, & a fresh new canvas for edgy copy.

Ideation

The main concept pushed for by 2AM was a clean, minimal, utilitarian, modern Japanese-esque design language, with a heavy emphasis on "not-stealing" Adererror's look.

However, to the designer's dismay (me), Adererror is too well designed, with its visual identity having so little to work with & so much claimed at the same time. Thus, we can't just jack A's shit. To truly construct 2AM to be the stylistic statement it aims to be, we will venture further.

Besides from these talented artists' work, I was also very inspired by the folks at Nothing during their pre-2025 branding, which pushed a similar aesthetic but with many physical world touches.

Absolutely stunning! Nothing better than to start to a project than a key visual inspiration, & a bunch of amazing works by people more talented than you!
