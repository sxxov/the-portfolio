Exposure

The brightness of the image. Controlling exposure is simply controlling the amount of light going into the camera.

Iris/Aperture

The physical size of the lens, measured in f-stop.

Shutter Speed

The amount of time the shutter is open for.

ISO

The sensitivity of the camera sensor itself.

Lens Perspective

The way light bends inside the lens with respect to its physical attributes (length, mostly).

Wide Angle

  • Focal length: 8mm - 35mm

Standard

  • Focal length: 35mm - 85mm

Telephoto

  • Focal length: 85mm - 200mm

Super Telephoto

  • Focal length: 200mm - 800mm

DSLR vs Smartphones

  • DSLR
    • Single purpose; built for only pictures/videos.
    • Large sensor size.
  • Smartphones
    • Multi purpose.
    • Small sensor size.
