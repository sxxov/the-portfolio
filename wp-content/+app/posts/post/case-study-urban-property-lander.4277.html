People search for homes, & hence homes need to exist beyond where they're built--on the web. It is here that sales meet aesthetics, where UI meets UX, where branding meets KPI; to masterfully homologise the art of the web & the art of the deal.

In this case study, I explore what it may look like to have a super clean, easy-to-use, & more importantly, SEO-potent site, for a developing project in the heart of Kuala Lumpur.
